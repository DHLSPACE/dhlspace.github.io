                        <h1 align="center" style="text-align:center;text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">前</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">&nbsp;</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">言</span>&nbsp;
</h1>
<p class="MsoNormal" align="justify" style="text-indent:2em;text-align:justify;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>根据教育部及四川省关于成人高校招生的相关文件要求，为方便考生填报在川招生成人高校及专业志愿，现将《四川省</span><span>2026年成人高校招生专业目录》（以下简称《专业目录》）予以公布，并就有关事项说明如下：</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">一、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">我省成人高校</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">招生</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">报名分</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">为</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">网上报名</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（含填报志</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">愿）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">、报名资格审核（含线上审核、线下现场审核）、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">网上缴费</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">三个阶段。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">其中，</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">网上报名</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（含填报志愿）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">时间</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">为</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">8月31日至9月5日，每天9:00—22:00</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">报名资格审核结果查询时间</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">自</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">9月14日9:00起</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">线下现场</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">审核时间</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">为</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">9月17日至9月21日</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">网上缴费</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">时间为</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">9月14日至22日，每天9:00-22:00</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">二、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">成人高校</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">招生</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">计划</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">分</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">两</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">个</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">阶段</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">向考生</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">公布，</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">第一</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">阶段</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">公布</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">各成人高校在</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">川</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">招生专业目录</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">第二阶段</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">公布</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">成人高校各专业</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">在川</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">招生计划数</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">三、报考成人高校医学门类专业的考生应具备以下条件：</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">1.</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">报考临床医学、口腔医学、预防医学、中医学等临床类专业的人员，应当取得以下三项条件之一：取得省级卫生健康行政部门颁发的相应类别的执业助理医师及以上资格证书；取得国家认可的普通中专及以上相应专业学历；取得县级及以上卫生健康行政部门颁发的乡村医生执业证书并具有中专学历或中专水平证书。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">2. </span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">报考护理学专业的人员应当取得省级卫生健康行政部门颁发的执业护士证书。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">3．</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">报考医学门类其他专业的人员应当是从事卫生、医药行业工作的在职专业技术人员。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">4．</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考生报考的专业原则上应当与所从事的专业对口。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">**特别提醒**：</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">根据原卫生部有关要求，医学成人高等教育学历文凭不能作为参加执业医师、执业护士考试的依据。拟报考临床医学、口腔医学、预防医学、中医学等临床类专业和护理学专业的考生，尤其是不具备上述成人高校医学门类专业报考条件的考生要慎重填报志愿。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">四</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>参加</span><span>“</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">选聘高校毕业生到</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>村任职</span><span>”“三支一扶”</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（支教、支农、支医和扶贫）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">“大学生志愿服务西部计划”“农村义务教育阶段学校教师特设岗位计划”等项目服务期满并考核合格的普通高职（专科）毕业生，凭身份证、普通高职（专科）毕业证、相关项目考核合格证，可申请免试就读</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">我</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">省</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">成人高校专升本。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>符合专升本报考条件的退役军人（自主就业退役士兵、自主择业军转干部、复员干部），凭身份证、退役证（义务兵</span><span>/士官退出现役证、军官转业证书、军官复</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">员证书）及相应的学历证书，可申请免试就读</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">我</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">省</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">成人高校专升本。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">五、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">《专业目录》</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">中</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">专科起点升本科</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">中</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">1</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">位</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">专升本</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考试码</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">为</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">专业对应</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考试编码</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">，</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">对应的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">统</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">科目</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">详</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">见</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">《</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">四川省</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">2026</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">年全国成人高校招生实施规定</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">》（</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>附件</span> <span>3</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">六</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">艺术、体育类专业术科加试的时间、地点</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">详见</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">《四川省</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">2026</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">年成人高校艺术、体育类专业加试科目一览表》。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:2em;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">七</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考生选报志愿，必须以《专业目录》公布的招生学校</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">、</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">专业名称</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">及</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">专业代号为准。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span>
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" align="right" style="text-align:right;text-indent:2em;">
