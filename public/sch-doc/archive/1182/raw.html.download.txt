                            <h3>研究生院关于2026-2027学年秋季学期研究生注册和上课的通知(the English Version is attached)</h3>
                            <div class="article-other">
                                <div class="article-otherBase">
                                                                        <span>
                                        <i class="iconfont icon-calendar-o"></i>2026.07.27
                                    </span>
                                                                                                            <span>
                                        <i class="iconfont icon-eye"></i>34240
                                    </span>
                                                                    </div>
                                <div class="share-btn">
                                    <span>分享至：</span>
                                    <span class="icon" id="shareToWeixin" style="background-color: #14BD03;"><i class="iconfont icon-weixin"></i></span>

                            <p class="layoutFV" style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">各学院、全体研究生同学：</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">根据《上海交通大学关于2026年暑假工作安排的通知》，现将2026-2027学年秋季学期研究生（含全日制、非全日制）注册和上课的有关事项通知如下：</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">一、注册时间和正式上课时间</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;"><strong>注册时间：</strong>2026年9月13日。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;"><strong>注册方式：</strong>&ldquo;交我办&rdquo;在线注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">全日制国内研究生（含港澳台）及全日制国际生必须到校、在校园内提交注册申请。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">非全日制研究生可以在校园外提交注册申请。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">如因人脸识别不通过无法在线完成注册的，请联系所在学院研究生教务老师或到学院研究生教务办完成线下注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">因特殊原因不能如期注册的，请提前向所在学院研究生教务老师办理请假手续；返校后应及时到学院研究生教务办进行线下注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">开课：</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">自2026年9月14日起开始上课，具体安排请参见个人课表，如有调整将另行通知。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">二、注册要求</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">所有在读研究生须在上述注册时间完成学期注册。已完成论文归档的2026年9月毕业生不进行学期注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">三、缴纳培养费</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">1、缴费对象</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">按规定需缴纳培养费的研究生，必须于注册前完成培养费缴纳手续。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">家庭经济困难的研究生可以按照国家规定申请助学贷款或者其它形式资助，并在办理有关手续后注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">已办理助学贷款的，可参照【六、绿色通道】要求办理相关手续后注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">2、缴费方式</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">请使用个人jAccount校园账号及密码，通过手机登录&ldquo;交我办&rdquo;App，点击&ldquo;财务-业务办理-在线缴费&rdquo;。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">四、毕业生</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">预计毕业时间为2026年9月30日且已完成论文归档的研究生无需进行学期注册；尚未完成论文归档的应进行学期注册；不能按期毕业的必须进行学期注册，同时办理延期手续（通过&ldquo;我的数字交大&rdquo;或&ldquo;交我办&rdquo;在线申请）。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">五、休学、复学</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">学籍状态为休学的学生无需注册；休学后申请复学的学生，需先办理复学手续（通过&ldquo;我的数字交大&rdquo;或&ldquo;交我办&rdquo;在线办理），再完成学期注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">六、绿色通道</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">已申请助学贷款但系统仍提示欠费无法注册的学生，需先缴纳学费超出助学贷款额度部分的金额（学费未超出助学贷款额度的，无需缴纳），经校学生事务中心审核、开通绿色通道后，再进行注册。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><strong><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">七、其它事项</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">1.医学院研究生注册、开课安排请根据医学院研究生院分院通知执行。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">2.各学院研究生教务办在注册时间段内，应随时关注学院注册率并及时核实未注册人员情况及未注册原因，于注册日后汇总数据报部门领导知悉。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">3.请各学院（系）必须高度重视研究生注册工作，及时通知研究生按学校规定办理学期注册手续。未按时完成学期注册的，将按照学籍管理规定处理。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5; text-align: right;" align="right"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">研究生院</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5; text-align: right;" align="right"><span style="font-family: simsun, serif;">2026年7月27日</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 2em; line-height: 1.5;" align="right">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5; text-align: center;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">Notice on Semester Registration of Graduate Students for 2026-2027 Autumn Semester</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">Dear respective schools and international graduate students,</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">According to the &ldquo;SJTU Notice on the summer holiday arrangement of 2026&rdquo;, the semester registration of graduate students for 2026-2027 Autumn semester is hereby notified as follows:</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">1.Date &amp; time for semester registration</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">Time: Sept 13th, 2026</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">2.Venue</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">The registration should be performed online via My SJTU (<a href="http://my.sjtu.edu.cn/">http://my.sjtu.edu.cn</a>, on smartphone it is交我办App.) For fulltime students, please complete your registration on SJTU campus. In case you cannot proceed with the online registration, please contact the graduate coordinator of your school or go to the graduate office of your school to complete the registration within the designated time frame.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; line-height: 1.5;"><span style="font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Helvetica Neue', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif;">If you are unable to register as scheduled due to special reasons please complete the leave procedure in advance at the graduate academic office of your school.             function utf16to8(str) {
                var out, i, len, c;
                out = "";
                len = str.length;
                for (i = 0; i < len; i++) {
                    c = str.charCodeAt(i);
                    if ((c >= 0x0001) && (c <= 0x007F)) {
                        out += str.charAt(i);
                    } else if (c > 0x07FF) {
                        out += String.fromCharCode(0xE0 | ((c >> 12) & 0x0F));
                        out += String.fromCharCode(0x80 | ((c >> 6) & 0x3F));
                        out += String.fromCharCode(0x80 | ((c >> 0) & 0x3F));
                    } else {
                        out += String.fromCharCode(0xC0 | ((c >> 6) & 0x1F));
                        out += String.fromCharCode(0x80 | ((c >> 0) & 0x3F));
                    }
                }
                return out;
            }
