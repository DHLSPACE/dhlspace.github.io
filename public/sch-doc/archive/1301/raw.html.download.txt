            <div><p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">浙江大学伊利诺伊大学厄巴纳香槟校区联合学院（以下简称“ZJUI学院”）将于</span></span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16.0pt" xml:lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2026</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年7月中下旬面向</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">全国优秀大学生（含外校、本校）举办夏令营。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">本次夏令营旨在搭建学术交流平台，帮助优秀大学生拓展学术视野、激发科研兴趣、了解学科前沿动态、增进对我院及导师的全面了解。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:12pt"><span style="line-height:24.0pt"><span style="font-family:宋体"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">欢迎全国（含外校、本校外院、本院）基础扎实、热爱科研、品学兼优、有意向报名我院学硕或专硕项目的优秀学生踊跃报名。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<h2 style="text-indent: 32pt; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">一、夏令营概况 </span></span></b></span></span></span></span></h2>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:12pt"><span style="line-height:24.0pt"><span style="font-family:宋体"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16.0pt" xml:lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2026</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年夏令营按学科专业和研究方向分为电子信息、人工智能1、人工智能2、电气工程、交通运输、机械工程、土木工程等七个分营进行报名。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<h2 style="text-indent: 32pt; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">三</span></span></b><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、申请资格</span></span></b></span></span></span></span></h2>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">欢迎符合以下条件的外校或本校2027届优秀本科毕业生（目前为三年级在校本科生）报名参加：</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16.0pt" xml:lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">1. </span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">拥护中国共产党，具有正确的政治方向，热爱祖国，遵纪守法；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16.0pt" xml:lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2. </span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">身心健康，诚实守信，学风端正，在校期间无违纪行为、未受过处分；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16.0pt" xml:lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">3. </span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">学业成绩优秀，对科学研究有浓厚兴趣，有较强的研究、创新能力及培养潜质，欢迎预期能获得母校推免资格的学生报名；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16.0pt" xml:lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">4. </span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">具有良好的英语水平。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"> </p>

<h2 style="text-indent: 32pt; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">四</span></span></b><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、申请方式</span></span></b></span></span></span></span></h2>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">网上报名：即日起至</span></span></span></span></span></span><strong><span style="color:#ffff00;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">7月2日</span></span></span></span></span></span></strong></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">进入浙江大学研究生招生服务系统（</span></span></span><a href="https://yjsy.zju.edu.cn/grsinfo/zs/user/login" style="color:blue; text-decoration:underline"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">https://yjsy.zju.edu.cn/grsinfo/zs/user/login</span></span></span></a><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">），注册登录后进行报名。具体操作详见附件“系统操作手册【学生】”。</span></span></span><span style="color:#ffff00;"><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">每人限报一个分营。</span></span></b></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">报名时请将以下材料打包为ZIP格式（不超过10M），文件命名为“</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">分营名称-在读院校名称-申请人姓名”（样例：人工智能1营-浙江大学-张三）</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">，上传到报名系统“附件材料”模块。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">1.个人身份证（正反面）复印件</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2.前5学期的本科成绩单（须加盖学校教务处或院系公章）</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">3.最新的专业成绩排名（须加盖学校教务处或院系公章）</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">4.英语水平证明</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">5.其他证明材料：包括能体现自身学术水平的学术论文、出版物或原创性工作成果、获奖证书等材料。请列清单，并提供主要的证</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">书等材料，请勿超过8页。这一类证明材料请合成为1个PDF文件。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请者应保证提交的所有材料的真实性、有效性和完整性，因不实信息或伪造材料所导致的一切后果由申请者本人承担。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"> </p>

<h2 style="text-indent: 32pt; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">五</span></span></b><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、材料审核与选拔</span></span></b></span></span></span></span></h2>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">我院组织专家对申请材料进行审核，择优选拔入营。名单将于7月8日左右在申请系统中公布，并以电子邮件形式通知入选同学。届时请入选同学按通知确认是否参加，逾期未确认视作放弃此资格。</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">未入选者不另行通知。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">1.夏令</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">营</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">不收取注册</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">费</span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2.夏令营期间活动安排会通过钉钉群发布。夏令营名单发布后请入选营员使用报名时提供的手机号注册钉钉，并及时关注钉钉消息。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">3.夏令营只招收有意向攻读我院硕士研究生的同学。学院常规推荐免试研究生招生报名工作将于8月上旬-9月中旬左右开展，届时请关注浙江大学研究生招生网与我院网站通知。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-indent:32.0pt; text-align:justify"><span style="color:#ffffff;"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="line-height:30.0pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri,sans-serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">4.夏令营活动以线上为主；如涉及线下活动（如导师组邀请来校进行短期暑研），营员需自行承担食宿及往返交通费用。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>
