    <h1 class="page-tit">西安交通大学与苏州国家实验室2027年联合培养博士生专项计划招生简章（直博项目）</h1>

<div id="vsb_content"><p style="margin-top:21px;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">为主动服务国家重大战略需求，培养和储备战略科技人才，探索以重大任务和目标为导向的博士研究生培养新机制，</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:21px">西安交通大学与</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">苏州</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:21px">国家</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">实验室</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（以下简称</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">“苏州实验室”）</span></span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">联合开展博士研究生联合培养计划</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">。为做好</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2027</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年专项计划招生，现</span></span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">根据</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:21px">西安交通大学</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、苏州实验室有关政策制定本招生简章。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">一、 </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:黑体">苏州实验室简介</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:42px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">苏州实验室是经中央批准成立的新型科研事业单位，位于苏州工业园区。苏州实验室强化战略性结构材料、功能材料和前沿材料的突破，打造材料领域大科学装置和</span>AI<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">计算设计平台，开展战略性、前瞻性、基础性研究工作，努力建成世界一流实验室。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">二、 </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:黑体">培养目标</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">聚焦国家重大战略需求，培养具有科学家精神、科技创新精神和创新能力，掌握前沿科学知识和技能，能够从事高水平科研和技术开发的拔尖创新人才。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">三、 </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:黑体">联合培养专项优势</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">1. </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">科研平台优势：可参加国家级科研项目、重大科学基础设施建设，为开展最前沿创新研究提供支撑。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">2. </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">师资指导优势：能够获得院士等高层次人才的直接指导，并有机会参与国际交流与合作。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">3. </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">待遇资助优势：具有竞争力的助研岗位津贴、生活补贴等。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">4. </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">职业发展优势：优秀毕业生可优先推荐在</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:21px">苏州</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">实验室工作。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-align:justify;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px">四、 </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:黑体">招生计划</span></span></p>
