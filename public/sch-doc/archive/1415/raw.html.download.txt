<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">科学仪器是支撑人才培养和科技创新的关键基础装备。当前，我国高端科学仪器国产化替代水平与应用规模亟需提升，物质测量领域复合型技术人才需求持续旺盛。为响应国家战略需求与行业人才缺口，设立</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">“物质测量学与技术”微专业，既是服务国家创新驱动发展战略、落实教育部“101计划”拔尖创新人才培养的重要举措，也是山东大学深化新工科建设、强化跨学科人才培养的重要实践</span></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">一、专业简介</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">“物质测量学与技术”微专业依托化学与化工学院高水平师资力量、公共技术平台大型仪器资源与成熟技术队伍，构建“基础理论-核心技术-综合实践-前沿创新”四位一体课程体系</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">。聚焦核心课程建设，强化课程内容与产业需求、前沿技术深度衔接，着力培育</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">学生实验设计能力、数据解析与创新思维</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">能力</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">，满足制造业、生物医药、新能源等</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">领域</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">对高精度分析测量、质量控制和设备研发的</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">人才</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">需求</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">，</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">培养</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">契合</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">学科交叉与产业升级的高层次专业人才。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">本微</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">专业呈现三大特色：其一，实践导向鲜明，课程体系深度融合仪器操作、数据统计分析与实际应用场景，通过标准化操作流程训练、复杂样品分析实训及科研项目实战，强化学生技术应用与问题解决能力。其二，学科交叉特性突出，构建多学科协同育人模式，培养学生跨领域知识迁移与综合运用能力。其三，技术前沿性显著，课程内容紧密</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">跟</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">踪仪器测量技术最新发展趋势，</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">确保</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">学生及时掌握行业技术动态与创新方向。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">二、培养目标</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">培养</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">基础理论扎实、实践能力突出、具备跨学科视野和仪器创新素养的复合型、创新型技术人才。学生将</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">系统</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">掌握精密测量技术、数据分析、仪器校准与质量控制等核心技能</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">及</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">设备研发</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">相关能力，</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">具备创新思维和解决实际工程问题的能力，能够从事测试技术研发、质量</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">管控、</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">设备</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">研制</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">等工作</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">，为国产科学仪器自主可控与高端化发展提供人才支撑。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">三、培养要求</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><strong><span style="font-family: 楷体_gb2312;font-size: 21px">（一）知识要求</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">掌握大型仪器分析表征技术的基础理论和专业知识。了解仪器测量学领域的前沿分析表征技术和大型仪器操作方法。熟悉实验技术相关规范、新型实验方法、新型检测标准等。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><strong><span style="font-family: 楷体_gb2312;font-size: 21px">（二）能力要求</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">具备独立开展科学研究和分析测量工作的能力。能够自主创新实验技术方法，可在大型仪器设备国产化研发领域取得创新性成果。具备解决分析测量技术问题、开展技术创新与组织技术研发工作的能力。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><strong><span style="font-family: 楷体_gb2312;font-size: 21px">（三）</span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 楷体_gb2312;font-size: 21px">素质要求</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">具备良好的职业素养和敬业精神。具备开阔的学术视野，立志深耕仪器分析测量领域一线工作。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">四、课程设置</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">课程设计遵循学科交叉和产教融合</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">理念</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">，依托化学分析、材料表征、计量科学等多学科交叉课程群，夯实学生跨维度知识基础；设置从仪器原理到应用创新的递进式实践模块，强化仪器分析测量技术实操能力与数据解析能力。通过产学研紧密结合的教育模式<span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">，</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">使学生</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"></span></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"></span></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px"></span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">精通仪器分析测量技术，具备跨学科</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">素养</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">与仪器创新研发能力，能应对复杂的分析测量挑战，在仪器分析测量</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">领域</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">发挥关键作用。</span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;text-justify:inter-ideograph;line-height:38px"><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">学业</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">表现突出、成绩优异的</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">学生</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312;font-size: 21px">，可优先推荐参与高端行业会议，获得优质实习岗位机会。</span></p>
