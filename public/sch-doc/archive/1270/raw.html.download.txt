复试总成绩大于等于</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">300</span></span></span><span style="font-family:宋体">分为复试合格，低于</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">300</span></span></span><span style="font-family:宋体">分为复试不合格，复试不合格者不予录取。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">本科同等学力考生须加试两门与报考专业和方向相关的本科主干课程。加试方式为笔试，加试不合格者即为复试不合格。</span><span style="color:#ff0000"><span style="font-family:宋体">每门科目总分</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">100</span></span></span></span><span style="color:#ff0000"><span style="font-family:宋体">分，及格分</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">60</span></span></span></span><span style="color:#ff0000"><span style="font-family:宋体">分。具体科目将以邮件通知相关专业的考生。</span></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">院系认为有必要时，可依程序对相关考生再次进行复试。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体"><strong>七、调剂复试</strong></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">本院各学科不接受调剂。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体"><strong>八、复试结果与拟录取名单确定</strong></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">请考生登录我校“研究生报考服务系统”查看结果。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">复试不合格的考生不予录取。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">一志愿报考我校的考生，将根据初试复试总成绩（初试复试总成绩</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">=</span></span></span><span style="font-family:宋体">初试成绩总分</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">×0.5+</span></span></span><span style="font-family:宋体">复试成绩总分</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman, serif">×0.5</span></span></span><span style="font-family:宋体">）进行排序，从高到低确定拟录取名单。</span><span style="color:#ff0000"><span style="font-family:宋体">一级学科的复试录取按研究方向进行分别排序。</span></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">若一志愿复试未完成招生计划，可能组织二轮复试；具体安排另行提前通知。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">进入“少数民族高层次骨干人才计划”和“退役大学生士兵专项计划”的复试考生，复试合格即拟录取。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">拟录取名单经审核通过后，由学校统一在网站上公示、公布。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">拟录取的非全日制定向就业考生，应在院系组织下签订定向培养协议书。拟录取的“少数民族高层次骨干人才计划”考生，应按有关规定签署定向就业协议书。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体"><strong>九、招生体检和复查</strong></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">学校招生体检与新生入学体检合并，即在新生复查期间进行。体检不合格的，取消入学资格或作相应处理。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体">入学后</span><span style="font-family:等线, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:宋体, serif">3</span></span></span><span style="font-family:宋体">个月内，学校还将按照《普通高等学校学生管理规定》有关要求，对所有新生进行复查，全面复查初试和复试全过程（含提交的所有资格审核材料）。一旦出现复查不合格，如考生学籍学历与初试前、复试时提交不一致，或在初试和复试中弄虚作假、违纪作弊等，将取消入学资格；情节严重的，将移交有关部门调查处理。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span style="font-family:宋体"><strong>十、信息公开与社会监督</strong></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0.74cm; margin-bottom: 0.28cm"><span
