        <h3 class="gp-f24 gpFontSize" id="shareTitle">化工与材料学院推荐优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生实施细则</h3>

            <span class="date">日期：2026-09-02 <i>|</i> </span> <span class="view gp-hide">访问量：<strong id="gpViewCount"><script async src="/cms/web/writeLog.jsp?siteID=2a18fca616d64bcca285a465dbbaf95d&channelID=a82391d7a7c842e6a0f64711b35e7190&contentID=8576c1b217c64d9882527f5ea1db6a71"></script></strong></span>

 <head></head>
 <body>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:36pt; text-align:center; line-height:150%; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:18pt"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-weight:bold">化工与材料学院推荐优秀应届本科毕业生</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:36pt; text-align:center; line-height:150%; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:18pt"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-weight:bold">免试攻读研究生</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-weight:bold">工作</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-weight:bold">实施细则</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">一、工作原则</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">根据《天津科技大学推荐优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生工作办法》（津科大发〔</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">202</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">6</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">〕</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">25</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt"> </span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">号）的文件精神，为加大学院拔尖创新人才选拔力度，本着公平、公正、公开的原则，结合学院实际，制定本细则。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">二、组织机构</span></p>

  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">5. </span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">学院推免结果公示（</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">2026</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">年</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">9</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">月</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">9</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">日至</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">2026</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">年</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">9</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">月</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">11</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">日）</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">。</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">学院推免生遴选工作</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">小</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">组召开会议，分专业依据综合成绩排名确定拟推荐候选人名单，将报名参加推免的全部申报</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">人员</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">的综合成绩及推荐结果在学院网站及学院公告栏公示</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">3</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">天。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">对有异议的学生，学院将查明情况，公布处理结果。如发现弄虚作假或作弊者，将取消其推荐生资格并追究相关人员责任。未经公示的，推荐结果无效。经学院公示结束无异议后，学院将拟推荐候选人名单及排名、拟推荐候选人全部材料（包括申请表、承诺书、成绩单、佐证材料等）上报教务处。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">6. </span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">学校推免结果公示（</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">2026</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">年</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">9</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">月</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">14</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">日至</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">2026</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">年</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">9</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">月</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">20</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">日）。</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">学</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">校推荐免试研究生遴选工作领导小组召开会议集中进行评议，确定推免名单，并公示</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">7</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">天。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">八、复议与监督</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">1.</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">已参加学院推免遴选，但对综合成绩和推荐结果有异议的，可在学院推荐名单公示期间提请学院推免生遴选工作小组复议。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">2.</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">推免资格候选人公示期间，接受社会监督和申诉，监督举报申诉电话：</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">022-60600275</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">、</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">022-60602735</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">3.</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">在公示期间，拟推荐人经举报核实或其他原因被取消或放弃推荐资格，则该名额指标根据该生所在专业的申请情况按照综合成绩择优递补，若所在专业无合适递补者，则该名额指标由</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">推免生遴选工作小组</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">研究决定分配给学院其他专业。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt">4.</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:10.5pt"> </span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">学院推免工作由学院纪委全程监督。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">九、取得推免生资格后，有下列情况之一者，取消其推免研究生资格</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">（一）经查实在推荐过程中存在弄虚作假、营私舞弊的；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">（二）受违法违纪处理中的；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">（三）报名参加全国硕士研究生招生统一考试的；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">（四）自行联系出国的；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">（五）自行就业的；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">（六）其他应当给予取消推荐资格的。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">十、</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">在</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">大三</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">年级</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">转专业的学生不具备推免资格。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">十一</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt">、</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">本</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">细则</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">未涉及的其他事宜，由学院推免工作领导小组研究决定。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:28pt; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">十二、本</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">细则</span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">从</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">2023 </span><span style="font-family:宋体; font-size:14pt; font-weight:bold">级学生开始执行，解释权归化工与材料学院推免生遴选工作小组。</span></p>
