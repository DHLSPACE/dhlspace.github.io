                                <span>2026-09-20</span>
                                <i></i>
                                <img src="/_upload/tpl/04/89/1161/template1161/images/mzw/icon-a4.png">

                            <div class="txt fs18">
                                <div class='wp_articlecontent'><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">根据《哈尔滨工业大学</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2027</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">年接收优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生工作办法》，已通过我校学院、学部、校区、未来工学院、地方研究院（以下简称“学院”）选拔并获得预录取资格的推免生，须在规定时间内登录教育部“全国推荐免试攻读研究生（免初试、转段）信息公开管理服务系统”（以下简称“全国推免服务系统”，网址：</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">http://yz.chsi.com.cn/tm</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">）完成报名及接收有关流程。具体流程提醒如下：</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;">一、推免生填报志愿</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">1.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">通过我校各学院组织的复试、获得预录取资格的推免生（含直博生，下同），须于</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">9</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">21</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">日</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">9:00</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">起登录“全国推免服务系统”填报志愿，并于</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">9</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">22</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">日</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">8:00</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">前完成填报。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">全国推免服务系统中填写的报名志愿须与学院通知的拟选拔志愿一致，包括层次（硕士</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">/</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">直博）、专业、研究方向（直博生导师）等。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;">二、推免生接受复试通知</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">1.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">我校将通过全国推免服务系统、电话或电子邮件等方式向考生发送复试相关信息。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2.9</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">22</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">日</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">8:00</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">起，我校陆续向正确填报志愿信息的推免生发送“复试通知”。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:31px;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">3.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">推免生须在各学院规定的时间内，及时接受我校发送的“复试通知”。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;">三、推免生接受待录取通知</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">1.9</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">24</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">日</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">8:00</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">起，各学院陆续向接受“复试通知”的推免生发送“待录取通知”。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2.9</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">24</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">日</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">14:00</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">起，收到“待录取通知”的推免生，须在学院规定的时间内及时接受我校发送的“待录取通知”。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;">四、重要提醒</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">1.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">未在规定时间内完成“复试通知”“待录取通知”接受确认的考生，视作自动放弃。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">请考生务必保持手机、邮箱畅通。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">3.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">已被我校接收的推免生，不得再报名参加当年全国硕士研究生招生考试，否则取消其推免录取资格。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">4.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">如申请人填写或提供虚假材料，或存在违反招生规定、有违学术道德、专业伦理等方面的不当行为，任何时候一经查实，即取消其复试、录取或读研的资格。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;">五、学费、基本奖助学金、住宿简介</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align:left;text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan;text-align:left;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">1.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">全日制学术学位研究生（不含中外合作办学、内地与港澳台地区合作办学研究生）学费标准为</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">8000</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">/</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">生</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">·</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">年；全日制专业学位硕士学费标准参见：</span><span lang="EN-US"><a href="https://hitgs.hit.edu.cn/2026/0920/c17352a401840/page.htm"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">https://hitgs.hit.edu.cn/2026/0920/c17352a401840/page.htm</span></a></span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">中外合作办学研究生、内地与港澳台地区合作办学研究生学费标准参见相关学院招生简介。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align:left;text-indent:31px;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan;text-align:left;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;"><span style="mso-spacerun:yes">&nbsp;</span>2.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">基本奖助学金参见《哈尔滨工业大学研究生基本奖助学金管理办法》</span><span lang="EN-US"><a href="http://hitgs.hit.edu.cn/2026/0727/c17343a398435/page.psp"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">http://hitgs.hit.edu.cn/2026/0727/c17343a398435/page.psp</span></a></span><span class="MsoHyperlink">。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:31px;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">我校研究生奖助学金体系还包括研究生国家奖学金、“三助”津贴和各类专项奖助学金等。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:43px;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">3.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">全日制硕士研究生可申请住宿，校本部原则上</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2-4</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">人间，住宿标准上限为</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">2000</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">·</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">生</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;">/</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:方正仿宋简体;">学年。住宿期限以基本修业年限为准，对超过基本修业年限的研究生，学校原则上不再安排住宿。校企联培学生住宿依据培养模式另行安排。威海校区、深圳校区结合校区实际情况，提供住宿保障。</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:31px;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;">六、院系联系方式</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:31px;line-height:37px;mso-line-height-rule:exactly;mso-pagination:widow-orphan"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:times new roman regular;"><a
