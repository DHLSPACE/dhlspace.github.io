                              <td class="font_black_14" id="zoom" height="450" valign="top"><div class=TRS_Editor><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">&nbsp;为多渠道促进我国高层次专门人才的成长，适应社会主义现代化建设的需要，根据《中华人民共和国学位条例》《中华人民共和国学位条例暂行实施办法》《国务院学位委员会关于授予具有研究生毕业同等学力人员硕士、博士学位的规定》等文件精神，结合我校实际，现将</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;&#10;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2023</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年接收同等学力人员申请硕士学位的相关事宜通知如下：<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:黑体">一、申请条件<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（一）拥护中国共产党的领导，拥护《中华人民共和国宪法》，具有正确的政治方向，热爱祖国，愿意为社会主义现代化建设服务，遵纪守法，品行端正。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（二）已获得学士学位（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:&#10;14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2020</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312;mso-ascii-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;&#10;mso-hansi-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;mso-bidi-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">年</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;&#10;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">9</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312;mso-ascii-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;mso-hansi-font-family:&#10;&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;mso-bidi-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">月</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;&#10;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">1</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312;mso-ascii-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;mso-hansi-font-family:&#10;&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;mso-bidi-font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">日前</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">）并在获得学士学位后工作</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;&#10;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">3</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年以上，在申请学位的专业或相近专业做出成绩。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（三）身体和心理健康状况符合国家规定的体检要求。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（四）经申请人所在单位同意。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（五）一般不接收小语种考生。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:黑体">二、申请流程<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请流程包括网上报名、申报备案、资格初审与复试、资格复审与信息采集、录取环节。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><b style="mso-bidi-font-weight:&#10;normal"><span <p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请前查看附录中《天津大学</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:&#10;14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2023</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年同等学力人员申请硕士学位招生目录》，登录“全国同等学力人员申请硕士学位管理工作信息平台”（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;&#10;mso-fareast-font-family:仿宋_GB2312">https://tdxl.chsi.com.cn/tdxlsqxt/index.html</span><span style="font-size:14.0pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">），依次完成“查看申请流程—上传电子照片—填写基本信息—提交学位申请”等流程。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0pt;mso-char-indent-count:2.0;&#10;line-height:20.0pt;mso-line-height-rule:exactly"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;&#10;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">其他个人信息请按照系统要求如实填写。如同时申报了本校两个专业，请在后期的信息采集阶段向学校申明，否则，申请人需对由此引起的后果承担责任。对于在申请过程中出现弄虚作假、违反考试纪律的考生，一经查实将永久取消其申请天津大学同等学力硕士资格，已被录取者将被取消入学资格。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
