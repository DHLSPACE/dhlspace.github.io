				<h3 id="shareTitle" class="fs30">中国农业大学2024年博士研究生招生章程</h3>
			</div>
			<div class="articleAuthor">
<span>发布单位：研究生院 </span>				<span>发布日期：2023-11-07</span>
				<span>浏览次数：<script src="/cms/web/writeLog.jsp?siteID=eccd42aeac684a11b1cff8d38005b490&channelID=0478561a29444baab1721cb232204d35&contentID=50db1b857aa547a29987763942dd830c"></script></span>
			</div>
			<div class="article fs18">
				<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">中国农业大学是我国现代农业高等教育的起源地和排头兵，其历史始于1905年成立的京师大学堂农科大学。学校于1995年进入首批“211工程”建设行列，于2004年被确定为“985工程”重点建设的高水平研究型大学，2017年入选一流大学建设高校（A类），2022年入选第二轮“双一流”建设高校。现已发展成为一所以农学、生命科学、农业工程和食品科学为特色和优势的研究型大学，形成了特色鲜明、优势互补的农业与生命科学、资源与环境科学、信息与计算机科学、农业工程与自动化科学、经济管理与社会科学等学科群。中国农业大学正朝着具有中国特色、农业特色的世界一流大学的目标阔步迈进，欢迎各位有志青年积极申报中国农业大学2024年博士研究生！</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">中国农业大学博士研究生招生分为三种方式：一是接收校内外获得推荐免试资格的优秀应届本科毕业生为直博生；二是选拔校内优秀在读硕士生为硕博连读生；三是面向校内外应届硕士毕业生或已经获得硕士或博士学位者，以申请考核制方式选拔录取为博士生（简称为申请考核）。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">一、招生专业及人数</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">2024</span><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">年我校博士研究生招生规模约1300人（含直博生和硕博连读生）。目前我校已完成推免直博生招生和硕博连读生选拔，申请考核制拟招生专业及人数详见《中国农业大学2024年博士研究生招生专业目录（申请考核制）》（附件1）。招生专业目录中公布的招生人数仅供参考，最后录取以教育部实际下达招生计划为准。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">二、学习方式与学制</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">我校博士研究生的学习方式分为全日制和非全日制两类。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">直博生学制为5年，硕博连读生博士阶段学制为3年，申请考核制博士生学制为4年，最长在校学习年限为学制加2年。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">三、直博生和硕博连读生招生办法</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（一）直博生接收办法</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">详见《</span><a href="http://yz.cau.edu.cn/art/2023/9/8/art_41740_979595.html" target="_blank" style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); line-height: 40px !important;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: #0070C0;">中国农业大学2024</span><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: #0070C0;">年接收推免生预报名通知</span></a><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">》及各招生院系实施细则。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（二）硕博连读生选拔办法</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">由各招生院系依据《中国农业大学硕博连读研究生选拔办法》（研生〔2019〕23号）组织实施硕博连读生的选拔工作。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（三）个人信息填报和材料提交</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">经过选拔拟录取的2024年硕博连读生，在学校规定时间内通过中国农业大学“</span><a href="https://yzk.cau.edu.cn/" target="_blank" style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); line-height: 40px !important;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: #0070C0;">博士研究生招生系统</span></a><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">”（建议使用360浏览器，极速模式https://yzk.cau.edu.cn/），完成注册和信息填报。直博生不需注册及信息填报，可直接登录系统，用户名：报名号（身份证号后十位），密码：本人身份证号。直博生和硕博连读生免缴报名费。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">直博生和硕博连读生通过系统自行打印“中国农业大学2024年报考攻读博士学位研究生登记表”，附两封纸质版“专家推荐信”和一份“思想政治情况表”（中国农业大学研究生招生网--资料下载），一并交招生院系，以便存入个人人事档案。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">四、申请考核制博士生招生办法</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（一）报名条件</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">1.</span><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">应届硕士毕业生（须在入学报到前取得硕士学位）或已获得硕士或博士学位者。其中：</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">（1）国（境）外获得硕士学位的考生需提供教育部留学服务中心出具的《国（境）外学历学位认证书》。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">（2）在读的“在职人员申请硕士学位”（只有硕士学位）的单证人员，不得以应届生身份报名，须获得硕士学位后方可报名。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">2.</span><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">符合招生院系对考生英语水平的基本要求（详见附件2）。</span></p>

<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">录取类别分为非定向就业和定向就业两类。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（一）非定向就业</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">非定向就业毕业后与用人单位双向选择就业。非定向就业考生和少数民族高层次骨干人才计划考生（定向单位为各省或自治区教育厅）入学报到前应将档案调入我校。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（二）定向就业</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">在职考生及报考非全日制专业的考生录取为定向就业。入学报到前考生本人、定向单位须与我校签订《定向就业协议书》，毕业后按协议回原单位工作。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">六、体检</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">拟录取的博士生应参加由学校统一安排的体检，体检医院为中国农业大学校医院。体检标准及收费按照国家有关规定执行。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">七、学费及奖助政策</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（一）学费</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">所有直博生、硕博连读生及申请考核制博士生均须缴纳学费。全日制学费标准为1万元/学年，直博生一年级按硕士生标准执行，直博生二年级及以后年级、进入博士研究生阶段的硕博连读生按博士生标准执行。非全日制土木水利、生物与医药学费标准为2万元/学年，其他非全日制专业学费标准以上级备案及审批结果为准。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（二）奖助</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">我校设置有完备的奖助体系，包括国家助学金、学校助学金、学业奖学金、国家奖学金、校长奖学金、专项奖学金、“三助”津贴及国家助学贷款等。针对直博生和硕博连读生，我校有倾斜性的奖助办法，详见《中国农业大学研究生教育收费及奖助体系实施办法》。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">八、培养地点及住宿情况</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（一）培养地点</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">我校博士生培养地点包括校本部（东校区和西校区）和三亚研究院。海南专项博士生的课程学习和实习实践主要在三亚研究院完成。两地执行相同的培养标准、毕业标准和授予学位标准，发放相同的毕业证书和学位证书。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">上一年度海南专项招生情况见《中国农业大学</span><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">2023</span><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">年海南专项博士研究生招生专业和人数统计<span style="font-size: 16px; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">》（供参考，附件3）。根据教育部2024年招生计划下达情况，可能会有新的学院和专业加入海南专项，具体招生学院、专业和人数以复核前学院通知为准。</span></span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（二）住宿情况</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">我校为基本学制内的全日制非定向博士生和非在职的少数民族高层次骨干人才计划学生提供住宿。其他定向就业博士研究生不提供校本部住宿，如录取为海南专项，可安排在三亚研究院住宿。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><strong><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">九、其他</span></strong></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（一）联系方式</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">学校实行校、院（招生院系）二级管理。考生在报名、复核、录取过程中的相关问题可先与报考的招生院系联系咨询，各院系联系方式请点击：</span><a href="http://yz.cau.edu.cn/col/col41739/index.html" target="_blank" style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); line-height: 40px !important;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: #0070C0;">联系我们</span></a><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 40px !important; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black;">研究生院招生办联系人：韩老师，咨询电话：010-62731800。</span></p> 
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 37px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: 微软雅黑, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif, 宋体; text-wrap: wrap; text-align: justify; text-indent: 37px; background: white;"><span style="font-size: 20px; font-family: 仿宋_GB2312; color: black; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; line-height: 40px !important;">（二）我校不提供往年复核试题、不举办考前辅导班。</span></p>
