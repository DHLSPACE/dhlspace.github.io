<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">各位同学：</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span style="font-size: 16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;中国科学院大连化学物理研究所将于<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">2026</span>年<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">7</span>月<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">13</span><span style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">日<span lang="EN-US">-18</span></span>日在海滨城市大连举办<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">2026</span>年<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">DICP</span>大学生夏令营。请认真阅读以下内容，按照要求和时间节点完成各步骤。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><strong><span style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">一、营员名单</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span style="font-size: 16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;我所<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">2026</span>年夏令营入营名单详见附件。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span style="font-size: 16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;请入营同学分别按照通知名单，于<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">6</span>月<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">29</span>日<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">12:00</span>前加入<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">2026</span>夏令营营员<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">QQ</span>群，<strong>群号<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">1038819708</span>，非研招办<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">QQ</span>群。</strong></span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span style="font-size: 16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;进群后，根据名单认真修改本人群备注（<strong>名单序号<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">+</span>报考专业<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">+</span>姓名</strong>），后续夏令营相关通知将在群内发布。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><strong><span style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">二、提交材料</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span style="font-size: 16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;请确定入营的同学，于<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">6</span>月<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">30</span>日<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">12:00</span>前发送以下文件<strong>至指定邮箱（<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">1746503576@qq.com</span>）</strong>：</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303;border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0"> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">.</span><span style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">《夏令营申请个人信息表》（签字及盖章；如有特殊情况，可先交未盖章申请表，并通过邮件说明具体情况即可）和本科成绩两个材料，合并成<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">1</span>个<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">pdf</span>文件<strong>，邮件标题、文件名称严格以“序号<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">-</span>报名专业<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">-</span>姓名”命名，例如“<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">1-</span>物理化学<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">II-</span>刘恒”</strong>。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303;border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0"> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">.</span><span style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">车船票<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">/</span>机票同时提供来连车票<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">/</span>机票购买截图（截屏需显示姓名，命名方式<strong>“<span lang="EN-US" style="border: none windowtext 1px;padding:0">1-</span>物理化学<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">II-</span>刘恒<span lang="EN-US" style="border:none windowtext 1px;padding:0">-</span>车票”</strong>）。</span></p>
<p style="text-align:left;line-height: 32px;background: white"><strong><span style="font-size:16px;font-family:'微软雅黑',sans-serif;color:#030303">三、住宿安排</span></strong></p>
