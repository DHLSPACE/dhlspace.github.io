<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">中南</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">大学竭诚欢迎全国各高校优秀应届本科毕业生推荐免试来我校攻读</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">硕士</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">研究生</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">以下简称推免生</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">），</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">为做好</span>2026<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年推荐免试</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">硕士</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">研究生接收工作，现将预报名有关事宜通知如下。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family: 黑体;font-size: 21px">一、申请条件</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">一</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）拥护中国共产党的领导，愿为社会主义现代化建设服务，品德良好，遵纪守法，在校期间未受过任何纪律处分。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（二）</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">经教育部批准具有推荐免试攻读硕士学位研究生资格高校的</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">202</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">6</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">届优秀本科毕业生</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">，</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">且有望</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">取得所在学校推荐免试资格</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">三</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">学习成绩优异，综合素质高，有志于从事科学研究工作，有较强的研究能力。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">四</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）具有良好的心理素质，身体健康状况符合国家规定的体检标准。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family: 黑体;font-size: 21px">二、招生专业</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">除工商管理硕士</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">[1251]</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、公共管理硕士</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">[1252]</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、工程管理硕士中的工程管理</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">[1256</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">01</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">]</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、教育硕士中的教育管理</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">[045101]</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、体育硕士中的竞赛组织</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">[045203]</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">之外，我校其他学术型学位、专业型学位相关专业均可接收推免生，优先接收报考专业型学位相关专业的考生。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">我校推免生</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">预</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">报名信息采集系统中的招生专业仅供参考，具体推免生招生专业以公布的</span>2026<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年研究生推免招生专业目录为准，最终录取专业以</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">“</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">全国推荐免试攻读研究生信息公开管理服务系统</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">”</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">中录取确认为准。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family: 黑体;font-size: 21px">三、报名方式</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">202</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">5</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">年</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">8</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">月</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">1</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">日</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">开始可</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">通过</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">“中南大学推免生服务系统”</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">进行网上报名</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">。</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">报名</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">网址：</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">https://yjszsgl.csu.edu.cn/zsgl2026/tmsgl/default.aspx</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">。</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">二级单位根据报名实际情况，遴选考核人员。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family: 黑体;font-size: 21px">四、提交材料</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">申请人按照二级单位具体要求提交相应材料</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">，</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">提交材料有且不限于以下材料：</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（一）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">本人身份证和学生证；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">二</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">网上报名《申请表》一份；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">三</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">个人简历；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">四</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">学习成绩单及成绩排名证明（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">由</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">本科期间</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">所在学校或院系教务部门加盖公章）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">五</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">大学英语四级或六级考试成绩单；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">六</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">学术成果（包括公开发表论文、出版著作、获得专利、获得学术科技奖项、承担课题或者其他具有学术水平的工作成果）；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">七</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">获得的各类荣誉、表彰、奖励证书等</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">；</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（八）各二级单位要求的其他相关材料。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family: 黑体;font-size: 21px">五、其他说明</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">一</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">学校各二级单位</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">将根据上级政策开展考核选拔工作，具体时间及工作安排请关注</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">二级单位</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">通知。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">二</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）申请人取得</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">正式</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">推免资格后</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">在教育部规定时间内</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">登录教育部全国推免服务系统</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">进行</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">报名，</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">并</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">完成填报志愿，</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">我校将根据学校</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">推免录取</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">实际情况发送复试通知，学生</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">接受复试确认、接受待录取确认</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">后</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">，</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">方可正式完成推免录取。</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">推免服务系统开放时间请关注中国研究生招生信息网相关公告。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">三</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请人必须保证提交的申请信息及全部申请材料的真实性和准确性。对在推免过程中存在弄虚作假、有违学术道德和专业伦理等不当行为的，或存在其他严重影响推免过程和结果公平公正行为的，一经查实，资格无效。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">四</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">）本通知内容与教育主管部门日后下达文件不符的，以教育主管部门下达的文件为准。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;line-height:37px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">（五）</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">咨询方式：</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">各</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">二级单位</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">咨询电话</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">详</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">见</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">中南大学研究生招生网</span><span style="font-family: 仿宋_gb2312; font-size: 21px;">。</span></p>
