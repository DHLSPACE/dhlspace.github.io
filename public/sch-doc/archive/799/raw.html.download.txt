  <p>﻿</p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:15.6pt; text-align:center; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:18pt"><span style="font-family:方正小标宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">电子信息与自动化学院推荐</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-weight:bold">2027</span><span style="font-family:方正小标宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">届优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生工作实施细则</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:center; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第一章</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">总</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">则</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第一条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">推荐优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生工作（以下简称推免工作）是研究生多元招生体系的重要组成部分，是加强拔尖创新人才选拔，提高研究生招生质量的重要举措，也是促进和激励在校本科生勤奋学习、全面发展的有效机制。依据</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">《天津科技大学推荐优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生工作办法》（津科大发</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">〔</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">202</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">6</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">〕</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">25</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">号）</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">等文件精神，结合学院实际情况，特制定本推免工作实施细则。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第二条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">本</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">细则</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">所称的推免生，是指经过我校遴选和推荐，无需参加全国硕士研究生招生考试初试，直接进入申报招生单位研究生复试程序的优秀应届本科毕业生。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第三条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">推免工作坚持公平、公正、公开的原则，遵循规范、透明的推荐流程。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:center; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第二章</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">组织领导</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第四条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学院成立以党委书记和院长为组长，分管本科教学和学生工作的院领导为副组长，</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">包括</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学院学术委员会成员代表、研究生导师代表的</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学院</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">推免生遴选工作</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">小组</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">，成员</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">9</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">人。秘书由负责推免工作教学秘书和辅导员组成。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第五条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学院推免生遴选工作</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">小组</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">负责本学院推免工作具体组织实施，落实集体议事和决策制度、审议并表决拟推免名单等，</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">同时负责处理推荐过程中申请者提出的质疑和申诉。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第六条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">推免工作受纪检监察部门全程监督。学院纪委对学院推免工作进行全程监督。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:center; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第三章</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">遴选基本条件</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">七</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">条</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">遴选推荐条件</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（一）</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">热爱祖国，拥护中国共产党的领导，拥护社会主义制度，遵纪守法，无任何违法违纪受处分记录，思想品德考核不合格者不予推荐；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（二）</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学习目的明确，学风端正，诚实守信，无任何考试违纪或作弊处分情况，未出现剽窃他人学术成果等学术不端行为</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（三）</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">掌握本学科坚实的基础理论和系统的专业知识，具有较强的分析问题和解决问题的能力，具备较强的创新潜质和创新能力；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（四）</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">身体健康状况应达到教育部规定的招生体检标准；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（五）</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">推免平均学分绩点在本专业排名前</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">30%</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">且综合素质测评平均成绩在本专业排名前</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">50%</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（六）</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">参加过往届推免生遴选的不得推免研究生。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第八条</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">研究生支教团遴选基本条件参照以上要求执行。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:center; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">四</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">章</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">推免综合成绩计算办法</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第九条</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">推免综合成绩由学业成绩、素质与发展加分两部分组成，综合成绩</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">=</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学业成绩</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">+</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">素质与发展加分。学业成绩由推免平均学分绩点对应进行百分制折算，素质与发展最多加</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK"> </span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">10 </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">分。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">（一）学业成绩</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">1.</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">突出考察学生的一贯学业表现。注重并加强对学生本科阶段学习情况的过程性评价，将本科阶段学业成绩作为推免工作最基础的遴选指标，不再专门组织推免生资格遴选的考试（包括笔试、面试等）。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">2.</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">补考成绩、重修成绩、个性化选修课程成绩不纳入推免平均学分绩点计算，具体计算公式如下。</span></p>

  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">十九</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">条</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学院公示期间学生放弃推免资格或被取消推免资格，该名额由学校推免工作领导小组统筹安排。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第二十条</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">学院将拟推荐学生名单及材料上交教务处，校推免工作领导小组集体研究审定名单，在学校网站公示</span><span style="font-family:'Times New Roman'">7</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">日。</span></p>

  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:30pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:15pt"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">第二十</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">二</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-weight:bold">条</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK"> </span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK">执行回避制度。推免相关工作人员有近亲属或与其有其他关系，可能影响推免公正性的相关人员等报名参加本单位推免招生的应主动申请回避，有非直系亲属等报名参加推免招生的要主动报备。相关学生申请推免资格时也应主动向学校报备声明。对未按规定报备声明回避关系的推免相关工作人员，学校依规依纪严肃处理；对未按规定报备声明回避关系且影响推免过程和结果公平公正的学生，学校取消其推免资格。</span></p>
