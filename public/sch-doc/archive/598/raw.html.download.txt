	<h2 align="center"><span>电子信息与自动化学院关于教师节期间廉洁自律的工作提醒</span></h2>
</div>
<center>
</center>
<div  class="c_title">
<h3 align="center"><span style="font-family:方正兰亭黑; size:14px"></span></h3><br />
</div>
<center>
</center>
<div  class="date1" align="center">   发布日期：2026-09-10 </div>
 <hr class="hr" />
<div  class="wznr"> <font color="#000000"> <html>
 <head></head>
 <body>
  <div style="-aw-headerfooter-type:header-primary">
   <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:justify; widows:0; orphans:0; font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:Calibri; -aw-import:ignore"> </span></p>
  </div>
  <p style="margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: left; line-height: 28pt;"><span style="font-family: 方正仿宋_GBK; font-size: 16pt; text-align: justify;">全院全体教职工、党员干部：</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">值此第四十二个教师节来临之际，学院向辛勤耕耘在教学、科研、管理一线的全体教职员工致以节日的问候和崇高的敬意！教师节是弘扬尊师重教风尚的重要节日，也是检验师德师风、严守廉洁从教的关键节点。为严格落实学校《关于进一步加强教师节期间廉洁自律工作的通知》精神，持续筑牢师德师风防线，涵养风清气正的育人环境，现就我院教师节期间廉洁过节有关要求提醒如下：</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-left:31.9pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">一、</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">提高思想认识，筑牢师德思想根基</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">全体教职工要深入学习习近平总书记关于教师队伍建设、师德师风建设的重要论述，牢记“教师要成为大先生，做学生为学、为事、为人的示范”的殷切嘱托，深刻践行教育家精神，牢固树立师德师风是评价教师队伍素质第一标准的理念，把廉洁从教内化于心、外化于行，坚守为党育人、为国育才初心使命。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-left:31.9pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">二、</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">严守纪律底线，严格落实“八个严禁”</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-left:31.9pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family: 方正仿宋_GBK; font-size: 16pt; text-indent: 32pt;">全体党员干部及教职工要绷紧纪律规矩之弦，恪守新时代高校教师职业行为准则，自觉抵制节日歪风陋习，坚决做到：</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">1. 严禁以任何方式索要或接受学生及家长赠送的礼品、礼金、电子红包、购物卡、有价证券等财物，杜绝快递寄送等隐形变异方式收礼；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">2. 严禁通过家委会、家长群等渠道向家长收取、摊派与教师节相关的费用，或要求家长提供各类私人服务；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">3. 严禁接受学生及家长安排的宴请、旅游、健身、娱乐等活动，不得由家长报销应由个人承担的各类费用；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">4. 严禁以辅导、补课等名义向学生收取费用，不得利用教师身份向学生及家长推销教辅资料、商业保险及其他商品谋取私利；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">5. 严禁组织、参与有偿补课或违规兼职取酬；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">6. 严禁借教师节之机违规发放津贴、补贴、奖金或实物；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">7. 严禁公款购买赠送节礼、公款吃喝、公款旅游或组织高消费娱乐活动；</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">8. 严禁发生利用职务之便谋取不正当利益的其他行为。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">对教师节期间发生的各类违规违纪行为，坚持发现一起、上报一起，坚决依规依纪处置。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-left:31.9pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">三、</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">压实责任传导，做实廉洁教育提醒</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">学院将同步统筹做好新生入学、中秋、国庆等重要节点廉洁教育。各系</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">、</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">部门</span><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">要做好纪律要求的传达到位，组织教职工学习中央八项规定及其实施细则精神、《新时代高校教师职业行为十项准则》以及学校各项制度规定，把廉洁自律要求落实到每一位教职工。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:32pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt">希望全院教职工坚守高尚师德，严守廉洁从教底线，共同维护学院清正、文明、纯粹的育人氛围，度过一个风清气正、文明简朴的教师节。</span></p>
  <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:176pt; text-align:justify; line-height:28pt; widows:0; orphans:0"><span style="font-family:方正仿宋_GBK; font-size:16pt; -aw-import:ignore"> </span></p>
