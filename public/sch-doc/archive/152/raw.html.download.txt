 <h2 style="text-align:center">2021年DICP大学生夏令营——第二轮通知</h2>
            <div class="fbt">发布时间：2021年06月30日　栏目：硕士招生</div>
    <div id="vsb_content"><div class="v_news_content">
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">中国科学院大连化学物理研究所将于</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2021</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">12-15</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日在海滨城市大连举办</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2021</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">DICP</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">大学生夏令营。我所已完成对全国高校报名学生的选拔工作，营员通知将于</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">6</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">30</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日（周二）前通过邮件送达本人，请认真阅读邮件内容，按照要求和时间节点完成各步骤。</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"> </span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">现将夏令营的时间安排及注意事项公告如下：</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: calibri,sans-serif; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;"><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; 一、时间地点</span></strong></p>
<p style="margin: 0px 0px 0px 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2021</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">12-15</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日，大连化物所星海园区、英歌石园区（能源学院）</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: calibri,sans-serif; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;"><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; 二、住宿安排</span></strong></p>
<p style="margin: 0px 0px 0px 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">国航酒店（男生）、城际客缘酒店（女生），</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">11</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日入住，</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">16</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">12:00</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">前退房</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: calibri,sans-serif; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;"><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; 三、活动安排</span></strong></p>
<p style="margin: 0px 0px 0px 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">初步日程详见下表，具体安排以实际为准。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">四、注意事项</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">请确定入营的同学将《夏令营申请个人信息表》（签字及盖章）和本科成绩两个纸质版材料于</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">5</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日前邮寄至我所研究生部。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">于</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日（周四）前完成问卷星填写并提交（每人仅限一次）。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">3. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">参加暑期夏令营的学生必须遵守我所相关规定，按照统一安排进行工作和活动。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">4. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">报销来连单程路费（根据所在学校城市，按照一定标准报销，如乘坐火车需提供纸质报销票据，乘坐飞机需提供登机牌和行程单）。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">5. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">返程票请同学们自行提前购买。</span></p>
