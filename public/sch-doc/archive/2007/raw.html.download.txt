					<div class="trs_editor_view TRS_UEDITOR trs_paper_default trs_word"><p style="text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">中国科学院新疆理化技术研究所</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">202</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">6</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（含</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">直博生</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">新疆大学及</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"> </span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">热忱欢迎青年学子报考新疆理化技术研究所！</span></span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:28px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-weight:bold;font-size:19px">一、</span><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋_GB2312;font-size: 19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">报考条件及要求</span> </span></strong></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:28px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">（一）</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">报考中国科学院新疆理化技术研究所普通招考的博士研究生，需满足下列条件：</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">1.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">中华人民共和国公民。</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">2.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">拥护中国共产党的领导，具有正确的政治方向，热爱祖国，愿意为社会主义现代化建设服务，遵纪守法，品行端正。</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">3.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">考生的学位必须符合下列条件之一：</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">(1)</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">已获得国家承认的硕士或博士学位的人员；</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">(2)</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">国家承认学历的应届硕士毕业生（能在博士入学报到时间前取得硕士学位，即</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2026年9月1日前须获得硕士学位）；</span></span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">4.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">身体健康状况符合我所规定的体检要求。</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">5.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">有至少两名所报考学科专业领域内的教授（或相当专业技术职称的专家）的书面推荐意见。</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">6.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">持境外大学硕士学位证书者，须通过教育部留学服务中心认证，提交认证报告。境外在读尚未获得硕士学位的考生须提供就读学校出具的在学证明（写明预计获硕士学位时间）。若被录取，在报到时须提供硕士学位证书和教育部留学服务中心认证报告，否则不予报到注册。</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:37px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">7.</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">自</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2026年招生起，招收的博士研究生除少数民族高层次骨干人才计划</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">民族药</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">专项外，原则上均为全日制非定向，停止招收在职定向博士研究生。</span></span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:28px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">（二）</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">应届硕士毕业生，最迟须在博士入学报到时间前取得硕士学位。</span> </span></p><p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:28px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric;line-height:33px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px">（三）</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">各专业均招收</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">“少数民族高层次骨干人才计划”博士研究</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">生</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">，</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋_GB2312;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">具体要求须符合教育部</span></span><span 		}

		$('form[name="searchforms"]').submit();
	});
