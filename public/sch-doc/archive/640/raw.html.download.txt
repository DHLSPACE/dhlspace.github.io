                        <p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">我省</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">2026年下半年</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">高等教育自学考试课程免试工作将</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">于近期组织实施</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">，现将有关事项通告如下。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">一、申请时间</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">202</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">6</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">年</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">9</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">月</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">17</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">日</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">9:00</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">至</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">21</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">日</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">17:00</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">，</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">逾期将不再受理</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">二、</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">申请对象</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">根据</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">《四川省高等教育自学考试课程免试规则（</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">2026年修订</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">）》（以下简称免试规则）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">的相关规定</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">，</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">已取得我省</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">自学考试考籍</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">且符合免试要求</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考生均</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">可</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">提出相应</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">课程</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">免试</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">申请</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">三、申请办法</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（一）</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">申请流程</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考生</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">在规定的时间</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">登录高等教育自学考试管理信息系统</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">以下简称</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">信息系统</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">考生端</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">网址</span><a href="https://zkk.sceea.cn/"><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>https://zk</span><span>.sceea.cn</span></span></a><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">，按照系统提示提交免试</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">申请</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">并缴纳审定费</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">，申请流程图见附件</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">1。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">根</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">据四川省发展和改革委员会</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">四川省财政厅《关于重新发布全省教育系统考试考务行政事业性收费的通知》</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>川发改价格〔</span><span>20</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">22</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>〕</span><span>4</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">84</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">号</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">）规定，免试审定费</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">8元</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">/科。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">（</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">二</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">）免试类型及申请材料</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">免试类型分为自学考试毕业证书类、其他学历证书类、非学历证书类和其他类型四个类型，每个类型需要提供的申请材料如下：</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">1.自学考试毕业证书类</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>（</span><span>1）</span></span><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>持</span><span>“2005年6月30日以后四川省自学考试毕业证书”的需提交</span></span></b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">有效居民身份证</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">原件。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>（</span><span>2）</span></span><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>持</span><span>“2005年6月30日及以前四川省自学考试毕业证书或外省自学考试毕业证书”的需提交</span></span></b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">中国高等教育学生信息网（以下简称学信网）</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">上可</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">查实</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">自学考试</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">毕业证书</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">原件；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">加盖公章</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">毕业生</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">成绩</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">档案</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">，并标识出用于免试的课程</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">有效居民身份证</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">原件。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">2.</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">其他</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">学历证书</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">类</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>（</span><span>1）</span></span><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>持</span><span>“毕业证书”的需提交</span></span></b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">学信网上可</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">查实</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">的毕业证书</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">原件；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">加盖公章</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">毕业生</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">成绩</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">档案</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">，并标识出用于免试的课程</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">有效居民身份证</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">原件。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>（</span><span>2）</span></span><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"><span>持</span><span>“结（肄）业证书”需提交</span></span></b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">学信网上可</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">查实</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">的本科</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">及以上结（肄）业证书</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">原件；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">加盖公章</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">的</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">成绩</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">档案</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">，并标识出用于免试的课程</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;">；</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">有效居民身份证</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">原件。</span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.1000pt;">
	<b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;">3.非学历证书类</span></b><b><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14.0000pt;"></span></b> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
	<span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;"><span>（</span><span>1）中国教育考试网可查实的笔试成绩报告单或合格证书（证明）原件。</span></span><span style="font-family:宋体;font-size:14pt;"></span> 
</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:28.0000pt;">
