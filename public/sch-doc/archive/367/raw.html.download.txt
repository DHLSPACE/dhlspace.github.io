<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">湖南大学竭诚欢迎全国各高校优秀应届本科毕业生推荐免试来我校攻读研究生。为做好</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2027</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年推荐免试研究生（含直博生）接收工作，现将预报名有关事宜通知如下。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">一、申请条件</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">1.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">中华人民共和国公民，拥护中国共产党的领导，遵纪守法，品德良好；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2027</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">届普通全日制本科毕业生，有望取得本科所在学校的推荐免试生资格；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">3.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">研究兴趣浓厚，有较强的创新意识、创新能力和专业能力；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">4.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">身</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">心</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">健康</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">，</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">符合国家</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">和我校</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">规定的体检要求；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">5.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">符合招生学院规定的其他申请条件。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">二、招生专业</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">接收类型包括推荐免试硕士研究生和直接攻读博士研究生。所有全日制招生专业均接收推荐免试硕士研究生，具有博士研究生招生资格的学院均接收直接攻读博士研究生。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">我校硕士研究生基本学制为</span>2-3<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年，具体以当年度专业培养方案为准。直博生基本学制为</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">5</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">三、申请流程</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">1.<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请人</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">8</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">月</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">28</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">日起可</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">登录</span></span><a href="https://yjszsxt.hnu.edu.cn/zsxt/tmsgl/default.aspx"><span style="text-decoration:underline;"><span style="font-family: 'times new roman';color: rgb(0, 0, 255);font-size: 21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">湖南大学接收推免生报名信息采集系统</span></span></span></a><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">，填写有关信息、上传附件材料并提交申请。报名</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">截止</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">时间以学院通知为准。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2.<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">学院将</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">分批审核申请信息，</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">根据</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">相关</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">政策</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">组织</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">开展考核选拔工作</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">。</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">具体安排请关注学院通知。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">3.</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请人须在</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">“</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">全国推荐免试攻读研究生（免初试、转段）信息公开管理服务系统</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">”</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">正式报名，完成填报志愿、接受复试确认、接受待录取确认等环节</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">。</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">服务系统开放时间请关注中国研究生招生信息网相关公告。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">四、申请材料</span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请人将以下材料按照</span>1-6<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">项顺序整理成一个</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">PDF</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">文件（不超过</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">10M</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">），上传推免生报名信息采集系统。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">1.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">个人简历、身份证和学生证；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">本科成绩单和总评成绩排名证明</span>(<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">由教务处盖章或所在学院盖章</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">)</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">3.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">英语水平证明材料（</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">如</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">CET-4<span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">CET-6</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">TOEFL</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">、</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">IELTS</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">成绩单等）；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">4.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">学术科研成果（公开发表的论文等）和获奖证书；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">5.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">参加社会实践、公益事业等的证明材料；</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">6.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">学院要求的其他材料。</span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:黑体;font-size:21px">五、注意事项</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">1.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">我校推免生报名信息采集系统中的招生专业仅供参考，具体推免生招生专业以公布的</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2027</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年研究生推免招生专业目录为准，最终录取专业以</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">“</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">全国推荐免试攻读研究生（免初试、转段）信息公开管理服务系统</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">”</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">中录取确认为准。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">直接攻读博士研究生招生专业、导师名单可参考《湖南大学</span>202</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">6</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年博士研究生招生专业目录》</span></span><span style="font-family: 'times new roman';font-size: 21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">。</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">如对招生专业、报考</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">博士生导师</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">等信息有疑问，可在报名阶段咨询相关学院。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">3.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">“</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">工程硕博士培养改革专项计划</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">”“</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">国家优秀中小学教师培养计划</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">”</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">招生专业及申请要求详见后续发布的相关报名通知。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">4.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">申请人应保证填报信息、申请材料的真实性和准确性。对于提供虚假报考信息或不符合申请条件的考生，一经查实，取消申请资格、考核资格等。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">5.</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">我校</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">研究生教育实行收费制度，各招生专业学费标准</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">以</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">公布</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">为准。</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">6.</span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">我校已构建完备的研究生奖助体系，支持研究生顺利完成学业。各类奖助学金评选发放标准以入学当年学校通知为准。</span></p>
<p style="margin-left:0;text-indent:43px;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric"><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">7.</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">研究生院网站将及时发布学校</span></span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px">2027</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">年接收推免生招生信息</span></span><span style="font-family:仿宋_gb2312;font-size:21px">，</span><span style="font-family:'times new roman';font-size:21px"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312">如上级部门出台新的招生政策，我校将做相应调整并及时予以公布。</span></span></p>
