                <div class="TRS_Editor">
                  <div class="trs_editor_view TRS_UEDITOR trs_paper_default trs_word"><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋">中国科学院山西煤炭化学研究所（以下称“山西煤化所”）是高技术基地型研究所，主要从事能源环境、先进材料和绿色化工三大领域的应用基础和高技术研究与开发。地处太原市中心，交通便利，环境优雅，住房条件宽松，并配有齐全的现代化仪器设备和丰富的科技情报资料。拥有太原桃南园区、小店园区；围绕煤炭高效低碳利用、炭基新材料制备与应用、氢能与燃料电池核心关键材料、污染物协同控制及废弃物资源化利用四个主攻方向，开展定向基础研究、关键核心技术攻关和重大系统集成创新；拥有含煤炭高效低碳利用全国重点实验室、煤炭间接液化国家工程研究中心、碳纤维制备技术国家工程研究中心等</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">5</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">个国家级研发单元；中国科学院（山西省）炭材料重点实验室、山西省粉煤气化工程研究中心等</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">5</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">个院、省级研发单元；主办有《燃料化学学报（中英文）》和《新型炭材料（中英文）》等学术刊物，均被《中文核心期刊要目总览》收录。</span></span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋">山西煤化所是</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">1981</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年国务院学位委员会批准的首批博士、硕士学位授予单位之一，设有化学、化学工程与技术、材料科学与工程</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">3</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">个一级学科硕士、博士研究生培养点；材料与化</span>工<span style="font-family:仿宋">、资源与环境</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">个全日制专业硕士授权点，共有在学研究生</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">400</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">余人。我所研究生全部享受学业奖学金，硕士</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">8000</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">元</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">/</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年，博士</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">13000</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">元</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">/</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年。研究生在学期间还享受助学金，硕士生补助不低于</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">30000</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">元</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">/</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年，博士生补助不低于</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">57000</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">元</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">/</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年，为研究生提供了良好的学习、科研和生活条件。</span></span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2026</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年我所计划招收博士研究生</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">53</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">名左右，含直博生</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">9</span><span style="font-size: 19px; font-family: 仿宋;">方式，具体</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">核发</span><span style="font-size: 19px; font-family: 仿宋;">方式</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">根据实际情况届时通知</span><span style="font-size: 19px; font-family: 仿宋;">。</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">准考证是</span><span style="font-size: 19px; font-family: 仿宋;">考生参加初试和复试的重要</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">凭证</span><span style="font-size: 19px; font-family: 仿宋;">，请考生一定要妥善保管直至录取结束。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">在复试阶段还将对报考资格进行复查，凡不符合报考条件的考生将不予录取，相关后果由考生本人承担。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">5</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">．</span></span></strong><strong><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">请考生认真阅读网上报名公告，网上报名时应务必认真准确填写并仔细核对本人的姓名、性别、民族、身份证号和报考类别（定向或非定向）等重要信息。报考信息和录取信息上报北京教育考试院和教育部后一律不得更改，学校也不再受理修改信息的申请。</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px">四、普通招考考试方式</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">1</span>．<span style="font-family:仿宋">普通招考实行“申请</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">-</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">考核”制。</span>人事教育处<span style="font-family:仿宋">根据《中国科学院大学博士研究生招生“申请</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">-</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">考核”制工作指导意见》和导师当年度已录取学生人数核定招生名额，向导师核发《报考导师初审表》。</span></span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2</span>．考生在我所网报截止时间时间（<span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2026</span>年<span style="font-family:Times New Roman">01</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">月</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">09</span>日）前，将个人简历、发表论文、获奖情况、能证明本人学术水平的其它材料，发送邮件至报考导师。</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">3</span>．导师将根据招生名额和考生情况，进行初选；并须在<span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2026</span>年<span style="font-family:Times New Roman">01</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">月</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">09</span>日前将《报考导师初审表》纸质版交至人事教育处。考生一经选定导师，直至录取结束不得更改。</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">4</span>．具体事项请见我所秋博报名公告。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px">五、考试时间</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent:37px;background:rgb(255,255,255)"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:仿宋">实行“申请</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">-</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">考核”制的综合考核暂定在</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2026</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">年</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">3</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">月，届时请考生留意我所官网通知。</span></span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px">六、体格检查</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">体检由山西煤化所<span style="font-family:仿宋">在复试阶段组织进行。体检标准参照教育部</span> <span style="font-family:仿宋">卫生部</span> <span style="font-family:仿宋">中国残联印发的《普通高等学校招生体检工作指导意见》（教学〔</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2003</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">〕</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">3</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">号）的要求、以及人力资源和社会保障部 教育部 卫生部《关于进一步规范入学和就业体检项目维护乙肝表面抗原携带者入学和就业权利的通知》（人社部发〔</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2010</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">〕</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">12</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">号）和《教育部办公厅 卫生部办公厅关于普通高等学校招生学生入学身体检查取消乙肝项目检测有关问题的通知》（教学厅〔</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2010</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">〕</span><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">号）要求进行，由</span>山西煤化所结合本单位实际情况提出具体的体检要求。新生入学后需进行体检复查。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px">七、录取和入学注册</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">1</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">．</span>山西煤化所根据下达的招生计划、考生入学考试的综合考评成绩（含考生提交资格审核材料、面试成绩、考生硕士或本科阶段的学习成绩、专家推荐书等材料的综合考评结果）、思想政治表现以及身体健康状况，择优确定拟录取名单。复试成绩或面试成绩不及格</span><span style="font-size: 19px; font-family: 仿宋;">（即低于</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">百分制的</span><span style=";font-family:&#39;Times New Roman&#39;;font-size:19px">60<span style="font-family:仿宋">分）</span></span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">的考生，不得录取。政审或体检不合格的考生也不予录取。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">2</span><span style="font-family:仿宋">．录取类别为“非定向”的普通招考考生，录取时必须转考生档案。未能将考生档案转至</span>山西煤化所的，取消录取和入学资格。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">3</span>．被录取的考生应在山西煤化所规定的时间内报到注册。如确有特殊原因不能按时报到者，须提供有关证明，且应以书面形式向山西煤化所请假，请假时间不得超过</span><span style=";font-family:&#39;Times New Roman&#39;;font-size:19px">10</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">个工作日；未请假或者请假逾期不报到者，除因不可抗力等正当事由以外，视为放弃入学资格。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">4</span>．被录取的应届硕士毕业生，应在入学报到时出具硕士学位证书原件。截止</span><span style=";font-family:&#39;Times New Roman&#39;;font-size:19px">202</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman">6</span>年</span><span style=";font-family:&#39;Times New Roman">5</span>．应届本科毕业生推荐免试录取为直博生的，应在入学报到时出具本科毕业证书和学士学位证书原件。截止</span><span style=";font-family:&#39;Times New Roman&#39;;font-size:19px">202</span><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span 37px"><strong><span style="font-family: 仿宋;font-size: 19px">八、收费</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">硕博连读转博考生经考核录取为博士生的，入学后按博士身份缴纳学费。</span></p><p style="text-indent: 37px"><span style=";font-family:仿宋;font-size:19px">直博生入学时按照博士研究生身份缴纳学费。</span></p><p             || str.indexOf('\\') != -1 || str.indexOf('/') != -1 || str.indexOf('"') != -1
            || str.indexOf('%') != -1 || str.indexOf('#') != -1){
            return false;
        }
        return true;
    }
    $(function(){
        $('#sub-pc').click(function(){
            var searchword = $.trim($('#searchword').val());
            if(searchword == "" || searchword == "请输入关键字" || !isValid(searchword)){
                alert("请输入关键词后再进行提交。");
                return false;
            }
            // $('input[name="keyword"]').val(encodeURI(searchword));
            $('form[name="searchform"]').submit();
        });
    });
</script>
