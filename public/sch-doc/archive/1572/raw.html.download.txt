    <h3 align="center">西北工业大学2025年硕士研究生招生简章</h3>

    <div id="vsb_content"><div class="v_news_content">
<p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">西北工业大学全面贯彻党的教育方针，坚定社会主义办学方向，坚持按需招生、全面衡量、择优录取和宁缺毋滥的招生原则。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-indent:32px;line-height:35px"><strong><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;font-weight:normal">一、招生计划</span></strong></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">2025</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">年我校拟招收全日制硕士研究生（含学术学位和专业学位）约</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">4800</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">名，非全日制硕士研究生约</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">900</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">名。招生方式包括全国统一考试、单独考试以及推荐免试，实际招生人数根据教育部正式下达的招生计划和当年生源情况调整。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">推荐免试研究生招生见西北工业大学研招网（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">http://yzb.nwpu.edu.cn</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">）公布的《西北工业大学</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">2025</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">年招收推荐免试攻读硕士学位研究生章程》，具有推荐免试资格的考生，可依据章程及院系具体要求进行网上报名、提交申请材料、完成各项报考流程。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-indent:32px;line-height:35px"><strong><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:黑体;font-weight:normal">二、报名条件</span></strong></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">（一）报名参加全国硕士研究生招生考试的人员，须符合下列条件：</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">1.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">中华人民共和国公民。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">2.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">拥护中国共产党的领导，品德良好，遵纪守法。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">3.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">身体健康状况符合国家和我校规定的体检要求。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">4.</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">考生学业水平必须符合下列条件之一：</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">1</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">）国家承认学历的应届本科毕业生（含普通高校、成人高校、普通高校举办的成人高等学历教育等应届本科毕业生）及自学考试和网络教育届时可毕业本科生。考生录取当年入学前必须取得国家承认的本科毕业证书或教育部留学服务中心出具的《国（境）外学历学位认证书》，否则录取资格无效。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">2</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">）具有国家承认的大学本科毕业学历的人员。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">3</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">）获得国家承认的高职高专毕业学历后满</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">2</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">年（毕业后到录取当年入学前，下同）或</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">2</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">年以上的人员，达到与大学本科毕业生同等学力，应提供由培养单位出具的与报考学科、专业相关的高职高专与本科之间相差课程进修的成绩证明，以及公开发表过的相当于报考学科、专业学士毕业论文水平的文章，报名时必须选择我校为报名点，经我校研究生招生办公室审核后方可参加考试，初试合格后，复试时需加试两门大学本科主干课程。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">（</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:21px;font-family:'times new roman','serif'">4</span><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">）国家承认学历的本科结业生，按本科毕业同等学力身份报考。</span></p><p style="margin-bottom:0;text-align:justify;text-justify: inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">（</span><span lang="EN-US" inter-ideograph;text-indent:43px;line-height:35px"><strong><span style="font-size:21px;font-family:仿宋_gb2312">未经网上确认，报考无效。</span></strong></p><p
