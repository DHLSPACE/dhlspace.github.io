    <h3 class="content-title" style="margin:10px; text-align: center;">四川大学2026年“工程硕博士培养改革专项”招生简章</h3>


<p class="add-time" style="text-align: center;">
    添加时间 2025/9/8 16:44 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;访问次数  37993

</p>

    <div class="content-box">

        <p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; 工程硕博士培养改革专项(以下简称&ldquo;专项&rdquo;)瞄准我国未来尖端科技和高技术领域，致力培养一支政治立场坚定、专业知识深厚、满足国家战略需求，且具备技术变革和产业创新潜力的高水平工程师队伍。</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span><span style="font-family:黑体"><span style="color:black">一、招生计划</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; 四川大学2026年在电子信息、机械、材料与化工、资源与环境、能源动力、土木水利、生物与医药七个工程专业学位类别招收工程硕博士培养改革专项计划硕士推免生和直博生共100余人。具体招生专业和联培企业可登陆报名系统查询。</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span><span style="font-family:黑体"><span style="color:black">二、报名条件</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">1.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">热爱祖国，拥护中国共产党的领导，拥护社会主义制度，遵纪守法，品德良好；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">2.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">具有（或预计可获得）所在高校推荐免试攻读研究生资格的2026年优秀应届本科毕业生。本科前三年学习总成绩原则上不低于所在专业前30%；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">3.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">身心健康，符合国家和我校规定的体检要求；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">4.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">工程技术或研究兴趣浓厚，有较强的专业能力、创新能力、协作能力；</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">5.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">符合招生单位提出的其他报名条件。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span><span style="font-family:黑体"><span style="color:black">三、报名方式</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">1.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">报名时间：按各考核小组规定的报名时间为准。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">2.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">报名网址：四川大学研究生招生信息网</span><u><a href="https://yz.scu.edu.cn/xly/login/" target="_blank"><span style="color:#2980b9">https://yz.scu.edu.cn/xly/login/</span></a></u></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span></span></span><span style="background-color:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">3.</span></span></span><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">网报流程：申请人登录网报系统进行注册（已参加夏令营者可直接登录），选择&ldquo;2026年工程硕博士培养改革专项&rdquo;，按提示进行网上报名和上传附件材料（包括本人身份证、学生证、近期免冠证件照、加盖教务处或院系公章的本科前三年学习成绩单、外语水平证明、有关获奖证书和科研能力证明材料等）。每人只能填报一个志愿。</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span><span style="font-family:黑体"><span style="color:black">四、选拔和拟录取</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; 招生单位将组织专人对申请人的报名材料进行严格的资格审查，由校企考核专家组按不低于招生计划120%的比例确定进入面试的申请人名单，并进行面试考核。参加面试考核的申请人名单和考核安排由招生单位另行通知。</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; 各校企考核专家组按联培企业和专业领域，根据申请人面试成绩由高往低依次确定拟录取名单。</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; 各校企考核专家组在面试的同时，还将对申请人进行思想政治素质和道德品质考查，考核不合格者，不予录取。</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; </span></span><span style="font-family:黑体"><span style="color:black">五、相关说明</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><span style="font-size:16.0pt"><span style="font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span style="color:black">&nbsp; 1.本专项录取的推免生均为全日制非定向工程类专业学位研究生，采用工学交替和校企联合的培养模式。硕士生学制3年，其中1年左右在学校学习公共课、专业基础课，2年左右在联合培养单位参加专业实践。直接攻博生学制5年，其中2年左右在学校学习公共课、专业基础课，3年左右在联合培养单位参加专业实践。</span></span></span></span></span></p>
