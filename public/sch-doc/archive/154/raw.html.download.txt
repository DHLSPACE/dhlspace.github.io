 <h2 style="text-align:center">大连化物所2021年大学生第2次夏令营通知</h2>
            <div class="fbt">发布时间：2021年07月19日　栏目：硕士招生</div>
    <div id="vsb_content"><div class="v_news_content">
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">各位同学：</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">为鼓励和支持大学生从事科学研究的兴趣和热情，中国科学院大连化学物理研究所预计将于</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2021</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">8</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2-3</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日举行大学生第</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">次夏令营（采用线上或线下形式）。现面向全国高校招收营员，并将相关的安排及注意事项公告如下：</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">一、申请条件</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">化学、化工、材料、物理、生物、环境、制药等相关专业的</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2018</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">级有意向来所读研的本科生（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2022</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月毕业）。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">学习成绩优异，<strong>预计能获得</strong></span><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">2022</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年推荐免试研究生资格的本科生</span></strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">。</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">二、奖励措施</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">1. </span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">我所将按照一定标准报销营员来程路费以及拟录取推免生的返程路费。</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">2. </span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">我所研究生在学期间实行奖助学金制度，根据攻读学位和科研课题的不同，博士生的奖助学金为5500</span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月以上（不设上限），硕士生的奖助学金为</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">3500-4000</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月，另有全覆盖的学业奖学金，硕士生平均</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">8000</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年，博士生平均</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">13000</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年。我所同时设立高额企业奖学金，国家奖学金等。</span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">三、报名时间及步骤</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">报名时间：</span><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">2021</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">7</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">19</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日——</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">7</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">22</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">组委会将根据提供的电子材料进行筛选，选中的同学再准备相关纸质版材料即可。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">报名步骤</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">）扫码或使用链接（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US"><a href="https://www.wjx.top/vj/Pk3Dp2r.aspx">https://www.wjx.top/vj/Pk3Dp2r.aspx</a></span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">）报名。</span></p>
<p style="text-align: left;"><img width="150" src="/__local/4/2C/63/1CDBE7C2A622C5CFA7414187A95_78631CB5_22CA.jpg" vsbhref="vurl" vurl="/_vsl/42C631CDBE7C2A622C5CFA7414187A95/78631CB5/22CA" vheight="" vwidth="150" orisrc="/__local/4/2C/63/1CDBE7C2A622C5CFA7414187A95_78631CB5_22CA.jpg" class="img_vsb_content"></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">）完成第一步报名的同学须将“个人信息表”（均以“学校全称</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">学院全称</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">专业全称</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">姓名”命名，如：大连理工大学</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">化工学院</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">化学工程与工艺</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">刘恒）填好后，发至</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">zhaosheng@dicp.ac.cn</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">发送的电子文档，不用签字和院系盖章。直接发送文档即可，只接收每位同学发送的第一次邮件，发送前请认真审核内容，完成以上两步骤视为报名成功。</span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">三、预计日程：</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">8</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日研究组自由参观，</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">8</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">3</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日综合面试，</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">8</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">4</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">日离所。</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">四、联系人：杨铎，</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">0411-84669170</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">，</span><span lang="EN-US"><a href="mailto:zhaosheng@dicp.ac.cn"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">zhaosheng@dicp.ac.cn</span></a></span></p>
