 <h2 style="text-align:center">大连化物所2022年大学生夏令营第一轮通知</h2>
            <div class="fbt">发布时间：2022年04月15日　栏目：硕士招生</div>
    <div id="vsb_content"><div class="v_news_content">
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">各位同学：</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">为鼓励和支持大学生从事科学研究的兴趣和热情，中国科学院大连化学物理研究所预计将于</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2022</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月中旬举行大学生夏令营（线下或线上形式待定，具体以实际通知为准），预计接收营员</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">300</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">名（往年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">200-250</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">名）。现面向全国高校招收营员，并将相关的安排及注意事项公告如下：</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">一、申请条件</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">化学、化工、材料、物理、生物、环境、制药等相关专业的</span><strong><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2019</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">级</span></strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">有意向来所读研的本科生（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2023</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">7</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月毕业）。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">学习成绩优异，<strong>预计能获得</strong></span><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">2023</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年推荐免试研究生资格的本科生</span></strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">。</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"> </span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">二、奖励措施</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">1. </span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">若线下组织，我所将按照一定标准报销营员来程路费以及拟录取推免生的返程路费。</span></strong></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><strong><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">2. </span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">我所研究生在学期间实行奖助学金制度，根据攻读学位和科研课题的不同，博士生的奖助学金为</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">5500</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月以上，硕士生的奖助学金为</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">3500-4000</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">月，另有全覆盖的学业奖学金，硕士生平均</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">8000</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年，博士生平均</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">13000</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">元</span><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">/</span></span></strong><strong><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年。我所同时设立高额的企业奖学金、国家奖学金等。开学后还有机会申请丰厚的<span style="color: red; font-family: 宋体;">入学奖学金</span>。</span></strong></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"> </span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">三、报名时间及步骤</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">1. </span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">报名时间：</span><span style="font-family: 宋体,SimSun;"><strong>2022</strong><strong>年4</strong><strong>月</strong><strong>15</strong><strong>日——6</strong><strong>月12</strong><strong>日</strong></span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">组委会将根据提供的电子材料进行筛选，选中的同学再准备相关纸质版材料（签字、盖章）即可。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2. </span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">报名步骤</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">1</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">）点击“<a href="https://www.wjx.top/vm/wFkDbDm.aspx" target="_self">报名通道</a>”或“扫码”报名。</span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><img width="150" src="/__local/5/6D/FF/105E15ADFF6091DF3D2DF0C8DBC_8F99CBE2_222A.jpg" vwidth="150" vheight="" vurl="/_vsl/56DFF105E15ADFF6091DF3D2DF0C8DBC/8F99CBE2/222A" vsbhref="vurl" orisrc="/__local/5/6D/FF/105E15ADFF6091DF3D2DF0C8DBC_8F99CBE2_222A.jpg" class="img_vsb_content"><span lang="EN-US"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></span></p>
<p style="text-indent: 28px;"><span style="font-family: 宋体;">（</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">）扫码报名的同学须完成“大连化物所</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">2022</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">年夏令营申请个人信息表”（均以“学校全称</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">学院全称</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">专业全称</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">姓名”命名，如：大连理工大学</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">化工学院</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">化学工程与工艺</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">-</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">刘恒）电子版的填写工作（暂时无需打印、签字和盖章），发送时间及方式待定。</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"> </span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: 宋体;">四、联系人：杨铎，</span><span style="font-family: Calibri;"><span lang="EN-US">0411-84669170</span></span><span style="font-family: 宋体;">，</span><span lang="EN-US"><a><span style="font-family: Calibri;">zhaosheng@dicp.ac.cn</span></a></span></p>
