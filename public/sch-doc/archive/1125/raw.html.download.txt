                              <td class="font_black_14" id="zoom" height="450" valign="top"><div class=TRS_Editor><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 24pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">天津大学专业学位博士研究生招生类别包含工程类专业学位博士（简称工程博士）以及风景园林专业学位博士两类。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin: 6pt 0cm; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 15pt; font-family: 华文中宋;">一、报考条件</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 12pt;"><o:p></o:p></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　（一）拥护中国共产党的领导，具有正确的政治方向，热爱祖国，愿意为社会主义现代化建设服务，遵纪守法，品行端正。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　（二）考生的学位必须符合下列<b>条件之一</b>：<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　<b>（<span lang="EN-US">1</span>）</b>已获硕士学位的人员（其中境外所获得学位须出具“教育部留学人员服务中心”的认证证书）；<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　<b>（<span lang="EN-US">2</span>）</b>应届硕士毕业生（最迟须在入学前取得硕士学位证或硕士毕业证），且硕士期间从事过工程实践类课题研究；<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　<b>（<span lang="EN-US">3</span>）</b>以硕士毕业同等学力身份报考的人员，须满足基本条件<b><u>并且</u></b>具有代表性科研成果，其中：<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;, serif;">&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">基本条件须符合以下<b>条件之一</b>：<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 30pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">&nbsp; &nbsp;①学士学位后具有<span lang="EN-US">6</span>年以上工作经历（从获学士学位之日起到<span lang="EN-US">2026</span>年<span lang="EN-US">9</span>月<span lang="EN-US">1</span>日）并且具有副高及以上职称；<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 30pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">&nbsp; &nbsp;②获学士学位后具有<span lang="EN-US">10</span>年以上工作经历（从获学士学位之日起到<span lang="EN-US">2026</span>年<span lang="EN-US">9</span>月<span lang="EN-US">1</span>日）。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;, serif;">&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">代表性科研成果须满足下列<b>条件之一</b>：<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 30pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">&nbsp; &nbsp;①在本职工作中已取得显著性成果，其中至少一项成果获省部级及以上奖励；</span><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 12pt;"><o:p></o:p></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　&nbsp; &nbsp; ②以主要完成人身份获得报考学院（部）认可的国家科学技术奖励工作办公室登记审批的《社会力量设立科学技术奖》奖励；<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　&nbsp; &nbsp; ③在报考领域以第一作者身份在我校相关学院（部）认定的核心期刊上发表的学术代表作<span lang="EN-US">2</span>篇。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　（三）具有较好的相关领域工程技术理论基础和较强的工程实践能力或潜力，能在国家重点行业、国家重大工程项目、战略性新兴产业行业中发挥技术骨干作用。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">　　（四）身体健康状况符合国家规定的体检标准，心理健康。<span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;<o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin: 6pt 0cm; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 15pt; font-family: 华文中宋;">二、招生类别和项目<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 24pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">（<span lang="EN-US">1</span>）专业学位类别：<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 24pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><b>工程博士：</b><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 12pt;">085400</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">电子信息；<span lang="EN-US">085500</span>机械；<span lang="EN-US">085600</span>材料与化工；<span lang="EN-US">085700</span>资源与环境；<span lang="EN-US">085800</span>能源动力（<span lang="EN-US">085801 </span>电气工程）；<span lang="EN-US">085900</span>土木水利；<span lang="EN-US">086000</span>生物与医药。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: 24pt; line-height: 23pt; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial;"><b>风景园林博士：</b><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 12pt;">086200</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">风景园林。<span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>
