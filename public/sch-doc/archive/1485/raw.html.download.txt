style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">1</span><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">、考核总成绩</span></p><p style="line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">（<span>1</span>）综合考核成绩</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">综合考核成绩分为四部分：英语（<span>1101</span>）、专业基础（<span>2999</span>）、专业综合（<span>3999</span>）和综合面试四个科目成绩进行加权求和的成绩，计算办法具体如下：</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">综合考核成绩<span>=</span>英语<span>×8%+</span>专业基础<span>×4%+</span>专业综合<span>×8%+</span>综合面试<span>×80%</span></span></p><p style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">（<span>2</span>）总成绩</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif"><span style="font-family: 微软雅黑, sans-serif; font-size: 19px; text-indent: 37px; text-wrap: wrap;">普通招考和专业博士</span>总成绩由申请材料审核成绩和综合考核成绩两部分组成。计算办法具体如下：</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">总成绩<span>=</span>申请材料审核成绩<span>×5%+</span>综合考核成绩<span>×95%</span></span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">两部分总成绩满分按<span> 100 </span>分计，并按四舍五入原则，保留两位小数。</span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif"><span style="font-family: 微软雅黑, sans-serif; font-size: 19px; text-indent: 37px; text-wrap: wrap;">硕博连读考生<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑, sans-serif; font-size: 19px; text-indent: 37px; text-wrap: wrap;">总成绩即为综合考核成绩。</span></span></span></p><p style="text-indent:37px;line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">若考生的任何一科或总成绩考核成绩不合格或体检不合格，均视为考核不合格，将不予录取。</span></p><p style="line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">2</span><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">、拟录取方案</span></p><p style="line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">（<span>1</span>）由具有本年度招生指标的博士生导师，根据报考考生的<span>“</span>总成绩<span>”</span>从高到低依次拟录取。</span></p><p style="line-height:27px"><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">①</span><span style="font-size:19px;font-family:&#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">首先按照（<span>1</span>）和（<span>2</span>）原则进行拟录取；</span><span wrap;">专业</span>博士考生，而不可再拟录取非定向类型的<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑, sans-serif; font-size: 19px; text-wrap: wrap;">专业</span>博士考生。</span></p><p style="line-height:27px"><span style="line-height:27px"><strong><span style="font-size:19px;font-family: &#39;微软雅黑&#39;,sans-serif">七、信息公开公示</span></strong></p><p style="line-height:27px"><span
